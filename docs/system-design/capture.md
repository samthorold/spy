# Capture

How an **Entry** gets into the notebook.

## Capture never waits on knowledge

An **Expression** can be saved with only its Spanish. Its **Meaning** is filled in later.

The material this notebook holds is organic: it arrives mid-conversation or from a class question, and the learner often catches the words before they know what they mean. If saving required a Meaning, the learner would have to stop and look it up in the moment, or lose the expression. Capture that waits on knowledge loses exactly the material the notebook exists for.

An Expression without a Meaning is still a full Entry. It is simply not recappable until its Meaning is known, because there is nothing to recall.

## Capture is free-form

An Entry is captured as free text, typed on the phone mid-conversation or on the laptop in class. Capture imposes no format: no fields, and no choice between Expression and Tip. Whatever the learner types is the Entry.

Free-form capture is affordable because structure no longer has to come from the learner at the moment of capture. Splitting a capture into Spanish, Meaning and Context, and telling an Expression from a Tip, can be done afterwards: by the learner, by an assistant (an LLM) whose suggestions the learner accepts, or by prompting the learner to review the day's Entries. Which of these the notebook offers is open. What is fixed is that none of them sits in the path of capture.

## Capture is minimal; everything else is enrichment

Capture asks only for what the learner has in the moment. **Meaning** and **Context** come through **Enrichment**, whenever the learner has time: after class, after the conversation, days later.

**Context** is free text, on any Entry. It is deliberately not split into fields such as register, region or source. Which details matter differs from one Entry to the next ("said when I mentioned I was about to cook", "informal, Spain only"), and a set of fields turns enrichment into form-filling, which is the friction this notebook avoids. Structure can be drawn out of Context later if recap or browsing turns out to need it; it is not designed in ahead of that need.

## Two kinds of entry

An Entry is an **Expression** or a **Tip**. They are separate kinds because only Expressions have a Meaning to recall: a Tip such as "in Spain they say *vale* where Latin America says *bueno*" is read, not drilled. Forcing a Tip into an Expression's Spanish-and-Meaning shape would either garble it into a fake translation pair or put it in front of the learner in recap with nothing to recall.
