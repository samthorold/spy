# Boundary

What the notebook is, as a system, and what sits outside it.

## One notebook, reached from every device

There is one notebook, held by a hosted service, and every device the learner uses (the phone in conversation, the laptop in class) is a window onto it. Capture happens on whichever device is to hand, and Enrichment and Recap happen wherever there is time, so all of them must see the same Entries. A notebook stored on each device would split the learner's Entries between devices, and Recap would only ever see part of what they have noted.

The notebook belongs to one learner. The service may later hold a notebook for each of several learners, but each notebook stays one learner's own; nothing here is shared between learners.

## Outside the boundary

- **The course and Lingvist.** Structured vocabulary, grammar and spaced repetition live there. The notebook holds what those do not: Spanish met organically in class questions and conversation.
- **Identity.** Who may open the notebook is delegated to an external identity provider rather than built here. Signing people in is a solved problem with real security stakes, and nothing about it is specific to learning Spanish.
- **Assistance.** Any suggested Meaning or Context comes from an external assistant (an LLM) and enters the notebook only as a suggestion the learner accepts (see [Capture](capture.md)).
