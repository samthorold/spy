# Spy

A personal notebook of the Spanish a learner meets in class and in conversation, with a light recap on top.

## Language

### The notebook

**Entry**:
Anything the learner notes down, in whatever words they typed. Every entry is either an **Expression** or a **Tip**, though which one may only become clear through **Enrichment**.
_Avoid_: card, note, flashcard

**Expression**:
An **Entry** holding a piece of Spanish someone used — anything from a single word to a whole phrase — and, once known, its **Meaning**.
_Avoid_: word, phrase, vocab (each names only part of the range)

**Meaning**:
What an **Expression** conveys, in the learner's own language. Often not a literal translation: *¡Buen provecho!* means "enjoy your meal".
_Avoid_: translation, answer, definition

**Tip**:
An **Entry** holding guidance about the language that has no **Meaning** to recall, such as *"in Spain they say vale where Latin America says bueno"*.
_Avoid_: rule, grammar note

**Context**:
Whatever the learner adds to an **Entry** to remember it by: the situation it came up in, who said it, how formal it is. Any Entry can carry it.
_Avoid_: notes, metadata, tags

**Enrichment**:
Adding to an **Entry** after it has been captured — its **Meaning**, its **Context**.
_Avoid_: editing, completing

### Going back over

**Recap**:
Going back over **Expressions** by recalling one side and revealing the other, in either direction: Spanish first or **Meaning** first.
_Avoid_: review, quiz, drill, study session

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** A native speaker says *¡Qué guay!* and the learner doesn't catch what it means. What gets saved?
> **Learner:** An **Expression** with just the Spanish. I add the **Meaning** later.
> **Dev:** And when the teacher explains that *vale* is Spain's *bueno*?
> **Learner:** That's a **Tip**. There's nothing to translate, it's guidance.

## Flagged ambiguities

- The code's `FlashCard` (question / answer) predates this language. An **Expression** is not a card: it has Spanish and a **Meaning**, and the Meaning may be missing.
